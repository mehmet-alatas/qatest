Test Scenarios

  1. Check if the page title is displayed as "Test Automation Form".
  2. Enter values with faker in "Username" and "Password" fields and submit the form. Check if the success message ("Hello [Username]! Operation successful.") is displayed in the correct format.
  3. Leave "Username" and "Password" fields empty and try to submit the form. Check if the warning message/red border is displayed.
  4. Select one of the gender radio buttons (e.g., "Male") and submit the form. Verify that the selection was made successfully.
  5. Select a country from the "Country" drop-down menu (e.g., "Turkey"). Verify that the selection was made successfully.
  6. Select multiple courses from the "Courses" section (e.g., "Java" and "SQL"). Verify that the selections were made successfully.
  7. Enter a comment in the "Comments" field. Verify that the entered comment was accepted successfully.
  8. Start the video on the page, then stop it, then verify that the submit button is interactive.
  9. Write a comment in the "Comments" field and verify that the text was submitted successfully
